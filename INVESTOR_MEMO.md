# INVESTOR_MEMO

## What this appears to be
`100scalppm` is a research strategy-factory for BTCUSDT intraday ideas. It generates many rule families, ranks them with a composite robustness score, and produces top-candidate reports.

## Why it may matter
The repository has a legitimate experimental framework and can be a useful **research asset** if governance and validation are tightened.

## Main risks
- Synthetic/limited data undermines external validity.
- Winner selection is exposed to multiple-testing bias.
- Top candidates are concentrated in near-duplicate breakout variants.
- Cost/execution modeling is too simple for institutional capital decisions.
- Some report artifacts may mix contexts (deep-dive vs top-ranking runs), risking narrative drift.

## Funding recommendation
Fund **only conditional research continuation** (no live allocation).

### Funding should be contingent on:
1. Real market data + execution-realistic simulator.
2. Formal false-discovery controls and walk-forward framework.
3. Diversity-constrained ranking and strict OOS governance.

## Bottom line
Promising prototype, not deployable alpha product.
